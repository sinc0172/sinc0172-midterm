Hi, I'm Millie!

I'm a passionate designer with a love for creating engaging user experiences and compelling visual content. A graduate of the Graphic Design program at Cambrian College, I dedicate my time to creating meaningful designs that resonate with users. I'm currently attending the Interactive Media Management program at Algonquin College, with the goal of improving my skills in video production and front-end development.

Outside of Design...

I love a good cup of coffee (latte's are my favourite!) and the sweet flavour of caramel. I also enjoy listening to all kinds of music, from VOCALOID, to classic rock, and everything in between. I have been unable to attend all the concerts I want to, but I hope to in the future! I often indulge in watching anime and reading manga in my spare time; I find lots of inspiration in these worlds. If I'm not playing Zelda, I'm likely losing hours building the perfect house in The Sims 4.
